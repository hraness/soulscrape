Portrait and emblem credits

Graphite illustrations for a curated collection of people, companies, and products, made from their source images. Portraits illustrate people; emblem studies illustrate an emblematic artifact or scene for companies and products, never a logo or trademark. They do not imply participation or endorsement by the subjects depicted or the image makers.
