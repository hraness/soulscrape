# Shared people, organizations, sources, and attributed dossiers

## Overview

Publishing a Soulscrape dossier should contribute to a shared, navigable knowledge network. A subject hub connects the dossiers about a person or organization, their cited sources, dated relationships, and attributed claims. Publishers retain their own interpretations. Shared identity is a navigation and evidence problem, not permission to blend several authors' descriptions into a definitive soul.

The user selected **shared hubs with attributed views** and **moving the full Hraness dossiers into Soulscrape** on 2026-10-04. Hraness will become a referring surface after content and deep-link parity pass. This document is the implementation plan and product specification. Phase status describes source implementation, never production activation unless the log supplies live evidence.

## Product model

### Records

1. **Subject:** a person or organization with a stable hub. A product, research lab, legal entity, and parent company must not be merged because they share a brand. Start with people and organizations; keep explicit product references distinct until a separately versioned product kind is supported.
2. **Identity assertion:** a publisher's claim that a dossier describes an external identifier or another subject. Store the method, originating publication, evidence, and current decision. A matching name, shared domain, or asserted QID alone is not verified identity.
3. **Dossier:** an attributed, dated view with its own scope, revision, source catalog, analysis, and omissions. Its author controls its publication. A hub does not assign one author's body to another.
4. **Source resource:** the cited URL/work used for discovery and backlinks. Its occurrences preserve original URLs, publisher-supplied dates and metadata, and capture/version digests when available. Sharing a URL does not mean sharing exact bytes or independent evidence.
5. **Assertion:** a publisher's claim or relationship, retaining claim kind, evidence, temporal scope, record ID, and dossier revision. `fact`, `stated_belief`, `pattern`, and `speculation` remain different. A fact label is not independent verification.
6. **Publication contribution:** the public subset of a revision that may enter shared discovery. Private drafts and personal annotations do not contribute counts, names, graph edges, source lookups, or existence signals to another user.

Subject hubs first provide a shared index of **publisher-asserted identity bindings**, clearly labeled as such. Compatible QID assertions may meet on the same identity-reference hub without merging dossier records or declaring the asserted identity verified. Conflicting kinds remain separate and visible; name-only matches never join across users. Durable reviewed entity bindings, redirects, and reversible split/merge decisions follow before stronger canonical-identity claims.

### Reader experience

A person hub shows their published dossiers, organizations and works mentioned in sourced records, and cited sources. An organization hub adds founding and operating history, people and their dated roles, institutional statements, repeated practices, products, and disagreements. The organization's model describes documented institutional behavior; it does not inherit its founders' private traits or imply unanimity among employees.

Each publisher page links to its subject hub. Each source links to the other public dossiers citing that resource. Relationships distinguish `works for`, `founded`, `was interviewed with`, and other supported meanings. A shared source or common topic produces a labeled discovery connection, never an invented social relationship. Every connection exposes why it exists and where its evidence lives.

For example, a published Steph Ango dossier and an Obsidian dossier can meet through a reviewed person binding, an attributed CEO role, and the same interview source. Another user's dossier contributes another view and its own citations. The interface retains differences in scope and time instead of replacing one view with the other.

### Automation

- At explicit publication, validate the packet and compute a deterministic, bounded contribution. Maintain subject/source memberships and cited-record references transactionally or through a durable, revision-fenced job. Publishing twice produces no duplicate memberships.
- Automatically connect exact source resources and compatible explicit identity references. Keep approximate/name-only matching as suggestions with reasons; do not silently merge identities.
- Replace a publisher's prior contribution on update. Withdrawal removes that contribution from all public discovery, including counts and backlinks. Other publishers' independent contributions remain.
- Discover missing subject stubs without manufacturing biographies. Do not publish a new person's dossier because their name occurs in another dossier.
- Within a user's private workspace, the same matching model may operate over explicitly selected records under that workspace's authorization. It never grants access to another user's private evidence.
- Research refresh can propose new findings and flag stale evidence. No automatic paid research, unrestricted crawling, or new public publication is enabled by indexing.

## Coupling decisions

| Boundary | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Soulscrape ↔ Oh | Reuse released research semantics through a versioned optional adapter. Keep dossier/privacy/publication policy in Soulscrape. Do not move the live service into the Oh kernel or use immutable Oh records as deletable private content storage |
| Sponge ↔ Soulscrape | Exchange selected, source-bound subject research and publication receipts. Sponge owns acquisition, execution, local memory, and reports; Soulscrape owns public subject publishing and shared discovery |
| Wordcell ↔ both | Keep Markdown and source artifacts usable independently. Include explicit public subject locators and source references in document metadata; no runtime imports from sibling checkouts |
| Hraness ↔ Soulscrape | Import full public dossiers with a checked mapping, then link/redirect admitted old routes only after destination parity. Keep Hraness-specific portfolio commentary on Hraness |
| Accounts ↔ entity identity | Accounts proves who may publish. It never proves who a dossier describes |
| PeopleBlade ↔ public graph | Reuse already reviewed public locators if explicitly selected. Contact matching, private communications, and private relationship inference remain local and are not seed data for the shared corpus |

Do not extract a new universal ontology service or require all products to coordinate releases. Prefer one released protocol, explicit adapters, and consumer contract fixtures. A shared package is justified only by two concrete consumers of the same tested semantics.

## Constraints

- Preserve existing `ensoul.*` and `soulscrape.person-index.v1` bytes, parsers, identifiers, and compatibility. New semantics get new versions; do not append unsupported keys to V1 packets.
- Keep the curated people Examples roster unchanged. Organization discovery is a separate first-class surface, not a silent change to that collection's policy.
- Unknown inputs are validated at every wire/storage boundary. Bound requests, profile counts, source counts, relation counts, serialized output, migration batches, and work per transaction. Overflow is explicit, not truncation presented as completeness.
- Source URL identity and content-version identity are separate. Preserve old source IDs; introduce a versioned URL grouping key rather than rewriting IDs whose preimage includes publication date. Do not equate mirrors, transcripts, or reposts without evidence.
- Keep private content out of the public-only packet. Do not infer publication permission from a valid schema or from an Oh packet's purported review/rights records.
- The existing public feed is not a snapshot or deletion log. Do not implement global resolution by loading the first page and assuming uniqueness, or by relying on `since` as a cursor.
- New shared indexes are derived from retained, attributed publications. Register every new table/route in `costs.json`. Keep large content out of new transactional indexes. Existing inline Convex packet storage is documented debt, not a reason to duplicate bodies into another graph store.
- Backfills are additive, resumable, and tied to source revision/digest. Keep migration readiness separate from activation. Preserve source packets and checkpoints; no reset, deletion, quota bypass, or forced identity merge.
- User-visible correction, withdrawal, impersonation reporting, and disputed identity handling must exist before broad shared discovery. A publisher cannot edit another publisher's dossier through a shared hub.
- Root check: `bun run check`. Site check, from `site/`: `bun run check`. Changed data surfaces also require root `bun run check:cost-surfaces`. Run broad gates through `host-run` on this host. Use focused test files in the edit loop.
- No commits, pushes, provider writes, releases, redirects, or content publication are implied by a passing source test. Record actual delivery evidence separately. Do not modify unrelated checkouts. Independent review must be recorded when required; do not call self-review independent.
