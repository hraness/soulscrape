# Soulscrape public site

Readers evaluate source-backed person research, inspect real example dossiers,
and install the skill. Keep the public evidence/private research/publishing
boundaries explicit. Product record content, permissions, publishing and dossier
interactions remain separate from this visual pass.

Use Nebula Sans, saved Paper/Gruvbox appearance, flat surfaces and one action
accent. The homepage proof is the real public example collection: portraits
and links open the source-backed dossiers. No floating, tilted or stacked paper
effects, decorative topic icons, backdrop textures or oversized serif display.
Keep portraits for identifying real examples; these are not fabricated UI.

The hero is one column, followed by ruled sections and simple use-case rows.
Navigation remains visible on phones, terminal examples scroll within their
container, and the shared organization footer follows content flow. Source
checks do not substitute for desktop/phone light/dark visual review. Preserve
saved appearance, keyboard access, no-JavaScript reading, and forced colors.

Shared design-kit0.23.0, site-footer0.20.0 and exact3df4 snapshot provenance are
committed together; local composition never edits immutable vendor bytes.
